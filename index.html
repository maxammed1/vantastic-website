Questions

Do you take wheelchairs?

Yes. Our vans have a ramp or lift and wheelchair tie-downs, and our drivers help you on and off.

Where do you go?

We pick up in Lake City, Wabasha, Plainview, Kellogg, Winona, St. Charles, Lewiston and nearby towns, and drive to appointments locally and in Rochester, Red Wing and La Crosse.

How do I pay?

You can pay yourself, or a hospital, care home, workers' comp or other program may pay for the ride. Call us and we'll tell you the price before you book.

Do you take Medical Assistance?

Call us and ask. We'll tell you whether your ride can be covered and how to set it up.

How far ahead should I book?

Two business days is best. Call us for same-day rides, like a ride home from the hospital, and we'll do our best.

Can someone ride with me?

Yes. A family member or helper can ride along. Tell us when you book.
