For hospitals, clinics, dialysis units and care homes

A ride partner you can call once

Discharge planners, social workers and care home staff use Vantastic to get patients and residents where they need to go, in a wheelchair van or a regular seat.

What you get

  • Discharge rides: call in the morning, ride home that day when a van is open.
  • Standing rides: dialysis and therapy on a fixed weekly schedule.
  • Monthly account: one invoice for all your residents' trips.
  • Rochester days: group Mayo appointments on the same van.
  • Trained drivers with background studies, and inspected, insured vans.
  • Trip records for every ride: pick-up, drop-off and times.

Prices are set before the ride. Ask us for our rate sheet.
